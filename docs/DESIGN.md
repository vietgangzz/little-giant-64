# Little Giant: Star Hop — design & asset contract

A Super-Mario-64-style 3D island platformer. The VGANG mascot **Little Giant** explores three
open levels of Vietnam: **Hạ Long Skies** (floating islands, rice-terrace plateaus and limestone
karsts over a bright sea), **Hạ Long Bay** and **Đà Nẵng – Hội An**. You collect bronze
**Đông Sơn stars** and **đồng xu** coins. Collecting every star of a level plays its finale and
the ending card.

Engines: **Blender 5.2** makes the character and props. **Godot 4.7.2** (Forward+) is the game.
Everything in Blender is generated by Python scripts in `blender/`. Run a script and it rebuilds
the `.blend` source file and exports the `.glb` that Godot imports.

## Units and axes

- 1 Blender unit = 1 Godot unit = 1 metre.
- Blender is Z-up. **Characters face −Y in Blender.** The glTF exporter turns that into +Z in Godot,
  which is the facing the player controller expects.
- Every model's origin sits at the **ground contact point**, the centre between the feet (props:
  centre of the base).

## Brand rules

- Body lime `#D5F64B` (sRGB). A slightly deeper `#C6DF70` also reads well under a warm key light.
- Eyes: forest `#234D37`. Only the two angled "gang eyes" from the brand vectors: no mouth, no
  blush, no pupils.
- Rays: orange `#FF7447`. They are three separate pieces floating off the top-right of the head.
- Hands are **detached nubs**: small balls of body colour, as in the brand drawings, like Rayman.
  Feet are two small rounded nubs under the body in the same idea.
- The silhouette comes from the approved brand vectors, kept verbatim as SVG paths in
  `blender/common/mascot_artwork.json`. Inflate it into a pebble. Never replace it with a sphere or
  capsule.
- Studio palette for UI: ink `#09090B`, burnt orange `#D15422` / `#FF8A4B`, gold, bronze.

## Mascot — `assets/models/mascot.glb`

Total height about **1.15 m**: feet soles at z=0, body bottom about 0.10, peak about 1.15.
Body is about 1.10 wide (x) and 0.70 deep (y). Eyes face −Y.

A short hero **cape** hangs from the upper back: the Vietnamese flag, red `#DA251D` with a yellow
5-point star. It is the cloth that shows spring-bone motion.
Its top edge must stay behind the flanks, so the eyes stay clear from the front.

### Skeleton (exact bone names, all deform unless noted)

```
root                  (non-deform, at origin; never animated by translation except in place)
└─ hips               body base, ~z 0.25
   └─ spine           ~z 0.55
      └─ head         ~z 0.85, tip at the peak
         ├─ eye.L / eye.R        scale-Y for blinks (eye meshes weighted 100%)
         ├─ ray.1 → ray.1.tip    each ray is a 2-bone chain (tip is short, deform=false ok)
         ├─ ray.2 → ray.2.tip
         ├─ ray.3 → ray.3.tip
         ├─ hand.L / hand.R      rigid hand nubs (parented to spine is also fine)
         └─ cape.L.1 → cape.L.2 → cape.L.3 ; cape.M.1→2→3 ; cape.R.1→2→3   (parented to spine)
└─ foot.L / foot.R    rigid foot nubs, children of root
```

The body is one soft mesh weighted by height across hips, spine and head. Squash and stretch
come from **bone scale** (glTF exports scale keys). The weights must blend smoothly, with no
hard seams.

### Animations (glTF animation names, exact)

| name | loop | length | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `idle` | yes | ~2.0 s | breathing squash, hands bob, occasional blink |
| `idle_look` | no | ~3.0 s | looks around (body twist), fidget; plays after idle for 6 s |
| `walk` | yes | ~0.8 s | feet step, body sways, hands swing opposite |
| `run` | yes | ~0.5 s | leaning forward, big hand pumps, bouncy |
| `jump` | no | ~0.35 s | anticipation stretch → up pose, hands up |
| `double_jump` | no | ~0.5 s | a full forward **flip** (360° about X) with a tuck |
| `fall` | yes | ~0.6 s | hands flail up, feet dangle |
| `land` | no | ~0.25 s | squash then recover |
| `dash` | no | ~0.35 s | lean hard forward, stretched, hands back |
| `ground_pound` | no | ~0.45 s | spin tuck in the air then point down |
| `ground_pound_land` | no | ~0.35 s | big squash, hands out |
| `wall_slide` | yes | ~0.6 s | optional |
| `hurt` | no | ~0.5 s | knocked back, eyes squint (eye scale) |
| `star_get` | no | ~2.2 s | spins, then holds a hand high: the Mario star pose |
| `dance` | yes | ~2.0 s | the ALL STARS celebration loop |
| `wave` | no | ~1.5 s | a friendly wave (title screen) |
| `sleep` | yes | ~3.0 s | slow breathing, eyes closed |

Animate the whole body in place. The controller moves the character, so there is no root motion.

glTF material `baseColor` + roughness. Godot overrides shading with its toon shader and keeps
the colours, so name materials by role: `M_Gold`, `M_Bronze`, `M_Leaf`, `M_Wood`…

## Godot side

- `project.godot` at the repo root. `blender/`, `docs/` and `tools/` carry `.gdignore`.
- The player is a `CharacterBody3D` whose capsule has radius 0.42 and height 1.1. The mascot scene
  is instanced under a `Model` node, and spring bones (`SpringBoneSimulator3D`) drive `ray.*`
  and `cape.*`.
- Each level is built in code at runtime: `World` (Hạ Long Skies), `HalongWorld` and
  `DanangWorld` in `scripts/world/`. Only the `Island` and `CaveDome` meshes are `@tool`, so they
  preview in the editor.
